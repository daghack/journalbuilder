They sit at one table, the thirteen, and no two of them sit together twice. I have counted the places and the places are always the same. When three of them rise they rise together and the other ten do not look up. I have written down every three that rose. Some of what I wrote is true and some was told to me by the one who watches from the middle of the table, and I can no longer tell which is which by looking. But the true ones keep the rule and the liars break it, and the rule does not lie. Every two of them, once. Never none. Never twice.
